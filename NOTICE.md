Xilinx ISE and the official VM are AMD/Xilinx products. Obtain them from AMD under their applicable terms. The Netatalk ISO contains no ISE installation, system disk, or Xilinx license file.

Xilinx ISE 与官方虚拟机属于 AMD/Xilinx 产品，请从官方获取并遵守相应条款。Netatalk ISO 不包含 ISE 安装程序、系统磁盘或 Xilinx 许可证文件。

## Netatalk 3.1.7

The offline disc contains a locally packaged Netatalk build, the corresponding upstream source archive and source RPM, build scripts, and upstream notices. The RPM identifies its license as GPLv2+. Preserve the disc's `licenses/` and `source/` contents when redistributing it; the upstream COPYING, COPYRIGHT, and AUTHORS files govern the third-party components. This repository does not replace those terms with a project-wide license.

离线光盘包含本地打包的 Netatalk、对应上游源码及源码 RPM、构建脚本和原始声明。RPM 的许可标记为 GPLv2+。再分发时保留光盘中的 `licenses/` 与 `source/`；第三方组件按其 COPYING、COPYRIGHT 和 AUTHORS 文件处理。
